Who We Are

Driven by Lived Experience, Focused on Long-Term Impact

Help Empower Refugees (HER) is a registered refugee-led CBO founded in 2024 and officially registered by the Turkana County Government in 2026. Based in Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement, Kenya, we work with refugees and host communities to build sustainable economic futures.

Our Core Mission

To empower women and youth with education, skills, and livelihood opportunities that improve lives, build self-reliance, and strengthen communities across Kalobeyei and beyond.
