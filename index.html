About HER

Locally Led. Community Driven.

Help Empower Refugees (HER) is a refugee-led, youth-led community-based organization based in Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement, Turkana West, Kenya.

Founded in 2024 and registered by the Turkana County Government in 2026, HER works with refugees and host communities to create practical opportunities that improve lives, strengthen local leadership and promote self-reliance.

Youth-led model

Young People at the Centre

Young people should not only benefit from development programmes — they should also lead them.

At HER, young people are central to governance, planning, implementation and decision-making. They serve as peer educators, trainers, community mobilizers and mentors, helping create programs that are locally relevant and community owned.
