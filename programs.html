Our work

Programs designed around community priorities

Program details are being developed with HER. The summaries below describe the organization’s intended focus areas and should be confirmed before publication.

Program details — confirmation required

01 · Building self-reliance

Livelihoods & Economic Empowerment

Supporting practical livelihood pathways, skills development and entrepreneurship for women, youth and households.

◉

Poultry & Livelihoods

Livelihood and food-security learning around poultry rearing, egg incubation and small-scale enterprise.

↗

Entrepreneurship & Skills Development

Practical learning that can support enterprise readiness, economic participation and confidence.

02 · Learning for opportunity

Education & Digital Skills

Creating welcoming spaces for learning, confidence-building and participation in a changing world.

▣

Digital Literacy

Practical computer and digital skills training that prepares youth for employment and participation in the digital economy.

♡

Social & Emotional Learning

Learning approaches that support wellbeing, connection and the skills to engage constructively with others.

03 · Resilience in action

Climate & Environment

Supporting community awareness and practical approaches to environmental protection and resilience.

♧

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Exploring sustainable approaches that can strengthen food security and environmental resilience.

◌

Climate Action & Environmental Protection

Community-led awareness, conservation and action for a healthier local environment.

04 · Health & wellbeing

SRHR

Community awareness and youth-led education on sexual and reproductive health and rights. This program area is intended to support informed discussion, peer learning and awareness of appropriate referral pathways.

Activities and delivery details to be confirmed by HER

05 · Leadership & inclusion

Women & Youth Empowerment

Supporting women and young people to contribute to community decisions, develop confidence, share their experience and take part in locally led solutions.

HER recognizes young people not only as beneficiaries, but also as peer educators, trainers, mobilizers and leaders.
