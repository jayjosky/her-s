Our Core Programs

Sustainable initiatives equipping women and youth with skills for economic survival.

Digital Literacy

Practical computer and digital skills training preparing refugee youth for modern employment and remote work opportunities in the global digital economy.

Poultry & Egg Incubation

Livelihood and food-security training in poultry rearing and egg incubation for households, micro-entrepreneurs, and youth groups.

SRHR Outreach

Community awareness and youth-led peer education focused on Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR) and gender equity.

HERHelp Empower Refugees

Building self-reliance and opportunity for refugees and host communities in Kalobeyei, Kenya.
