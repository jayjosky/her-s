About HER

Refugee-led and youth-driven solutions in Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement, Kenya.

Help Empower Refugees (HER) works alongside refugees and host communities to strengthen opportunity, local leadership and practical pathways to self-reliance through education, skills, livelihoods, climate action and community development.

Who We Are

Founded on Community & Experience

Help Empower Refugees (HER) is a registered refugee-led CBO founded in 2024 and officially registered by the Turkana County Government in 2026. Based in Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement, Kenya, we work with refugees and host communities to create practical opportunities that build self-reliance, income generation, and community well-being.

2024 Founded
2026 Government Registered

Our Vision

A world where everyone is empowered to thrive through self-reliance, leadership, and local innovation.

Our Mission

To empower women and youth with education, skills, and livelihood opportunities that improve lives, build self-reliance, and strengthen communities.

Why HER

Local Knowledge, Practical Action

HER brings lived experience, youth leadership and community knowledge to the work of creating practical opportunities in Kalobeyei.

As a registered CBO, HER is building partnerships grounded in local ownership, trust and long-term self-reliance.

Why partner with HER?

  • Refugee-led and youth-led perspective
  • Registered Community-Based Organization
  • Community knowledge and locally relevant approaches
  • Commitment to sustainable, community-owned outcomes
